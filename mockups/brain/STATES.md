## Decisions made in this mockup (approved by Morgan 2026-11-10)

1. Tools on the task card carry a word as well as an icon (Morgan worried icon-only buttons rely on hover).
2. While a sector is open the dock shrinks to just the command bar, like today.
3. Answers and the add-a-task card take over the log column and the dock grows taller while one is open, instead of floating over the brain.
4. On days with no class, "N done today" lives in the mission-log header.
5. Low energy, low power, the pinned jump and Plain version sit in the top bar.
